Where are the apartments located?

Both are at 3 Bouboulinas Street, Mouzaki, Karditsa — not in Trikala or Meteora itself.

Can I choose between NP-Apt and GP-Apt?

Yes. Compare amenities, explore the photos and select your preferred apartment listing.

Is the garden private?

No. The garden is shared between the two apartments.

How can I check current rates and dates?

Use the apartment's Booking.com or Airbnb listing. This website does not show live availability.

How do I receive check-in instructions?

Detailed access instructions are shared privately with confirmed guests before arrival.
