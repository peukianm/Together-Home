Meteora and Kalambaka: a day trip from Mouzaki

Give Meteora a full day, with time for a monastery, the landscape and a stroll in Kalambaka or Kastraki. When staying at NP-Apt or GP-Apt in Mouzaki, Karditsa, plan your trip by car and return to your apartment to relax.

Kalambaka, Kastraki or a particular monastery?

Kalambaka lies at the foot of Meteora, while Kastraki is another stop for exploring the landscape. The monasteries are in different locations, so choose a specific destination before leaving Mouzaki.

A relaxed day in Meteora

  1. Choose the visit that interests you mostBuild the day around the opening hours of the monastery you want to see. For a first visit, we suggest one or two monasteries, leaving time to appreciate the landscape.
  2. Take a break in KastrakiAllow time for coffee or a short walk. Choose the next stop to suit your group and the weather rather than trying to fit every viewpoint into one day.
  3. Spend time in KalambakaCombine the trip with a meal and a break in town. For families or visitors who prefer less walking, this stop makes the itinerary more flexible.
  4. Return to MouzakiKeep the return journey as relaxed as you need. Your apartment has a fitted kitchen and a separate space to unwind before the next day’s outing.

Monastery hours and practical planning

Meteora is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Monastery visits need planning because opening hours and visiting days differ. Consult the Greek National Tourism Organisation’s visitor information and confirm arrangements directly for your chosen visit.

Access to several monasteries includes steps and uphill walking. Choose a visit that suits your group and check the dress requirements. For landscape photography, use places where stopping and access are permitted.

Staying in Mouzaki for a Meteora trip

Are NP-Apt and GP-Apt in Kalambaka?

Both apartments are at 3 Bouboulinas Street, Mouzaki, Karditsa. Kalambaka, Kastraki and the monasteries are day-trip destinations and require travel.

Can I see all of Meteora in one day?

A day trip lets you experience the landscape and selected monasteries. For a more detailed visit, allow additional days and plan around the current opening hours.

Does the trip suit a family?

Choose fewer visits, account for steps and allow time for meals and breaks. Check the particular journey and access requirements before deciding on your itinerary.

How do I book a stay?

Open NP-Apt or GP-Apt, choose your dates and guests and calculate the indicative cost. Each apartment welcomes up to 4 guests with at least 1 adult and shared garden access. Send a request and we will contact you to finalize the reservation.
