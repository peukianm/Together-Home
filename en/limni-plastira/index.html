Lake Plastiras: a day trip from Mouzaki

Give Lake Plastiras a full day, with a few selected stops and time to enjoy the landscape. When staying at NP-Apt or GP-Apt in Mouzaki, Karditsa, you can plan a trip by car and return to your apartment in the afternoon.

A relaxed day between the lake and its villages

  1. Leave Mouzaki in the morningDecide on the two or three stops that matter most to you. For a first visit, we suggest one lakeside stop and one village, leaving room for short, spontaneous walks.
  2. Kalyvia Pezoulas and the lakeside landscapeChoose an accessible spot for a walk and photographs. If you want an organised activity, contact the provider before leaving to confirm hours and availability.
  3. Take a break in NeochoriThe village extends along the hillside and has views towards the lake. Allow time for a meal or coffee and a stroll, without feeling you have to complete the entire circuit of the lake.
  4. Return with time to relaxPlan your return around daylight, weather and the needs of your group. Back in Mouzaki, enjoy a relaxed evening and prepare for the next day’s outing.

Before setting out

Keep your plans flexible and allow time for breaks. With children, fewer stops and time for meals and rest can make the day more comfortable. Bring water, comfortable shoes and clothing suitable for the weather forecast.

Organised activities and local businesses have their own opening hours. Check official local information and contact providers directly for anything you want to book. Our suggestions are a flexible itinerary rather than an organised tour.

Staying in Mouzaki for a lake trip

Are the apartments beside Lake Plastiras?

NP-Apt and GP-Apt are in Mouzaki, Karditsa. The lake is a day-trip destination from the accommodation and requires travel. Check the route to the particular village you plan to visit.

Can I combine the lake with Trikala?

You can dedicate separate days to the lake and Trikala, starting from Mouzaki. This leaves more time for each destination and less pressure on your journeys.

What amenities do the apartments offer?

Each apartment has a fitted kitchen, Wi-Fi and air conditioning and welcomes up to 4 guests, with at least 1 adult. Access to the garden is shared. See the individual apartment pages for the full amenity details.

Where can I see the cost of my stay?

On the NP-Apt or GP-Apt page, choose your dates and guests in Availability & Booking. Calculate an indicative cost and send a request. We will contact you to finalize the reservation.
