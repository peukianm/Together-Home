Elati and Pertouli: a mountain day trip from Mouzaki

Plan a day in the mountains of Trikala, with a walk in Elati, a stop in the Pertouli meadows and time for Pertouli village. NP-Apt and GP-Apt in Mouzaki, Karditsa are your starting point and the place to return to after your outing.

A flexible day in Elati and Pertouli

  1. Start with the stop that suits youFor a fuller outing, add Pyli. If you prefer more time in the mountains, keep the day for Elati and Pertouli.
  2. Enjoy Elati on footLeave time for the village, coffee and local shops. It is an opportunity to slow down and decide whether you would like to continue with further stops.
  3. Choose the meadows or a walk in PertouliCombine the natural landscape with a village stop. For horse riding, a guided walk or another activity, contact the provider beforehand to confirm availability and the meeting point.
  4. Leave time for a meal and the return journeyKeep your day from becoming a sequence of drives. Plan your return around the weather and the pace of your group, then relax at your apartment in Mouzaki.

Adapt the trip to the season

Elati and Pertouli offer different reasons to visit throughout the year. Plan around the weather forecast and current road conditions. In winter, check access conditions before leaving.

Pertouli Ski Centre operates according to conditions and its announcements. If skiing is your aim, confirm that it is operating before building the day around it. For a walk, choose a route suited to the experience and needs of your group.

Before choosing your stay

Are the apartments in Elati or Pertouli?

NP-Apt and GP-Apt are in Mouzaki, Karditsa. Visiting the mountain villages requires travel from the accommodation. Check directions to each stop to decide whether the location suits you.

Do I need to visit every stop on the same day?

No. Choose the places that interest you most. For a relaxed day, combine one village with one stop in nature and keep the remaining options for another day.

Is there always snow and an opportunity to ski?

Snowfall and ski centre operation are not guaranteed. Check current announcements and adapt your outing to the conditions.

What do NP-Apt and GP-Apt offer?

Each apartment welcomes up to 4 guests with at least 1 adult and has a fitted kitchen, Wi-Fi and air conditioning. Garden access is shared. Choose dates on the apartment page, calculate an indicative cost and send a booking request.
