A suggested day out

  1. Begin with a walk in TrikalaStroll by the Lithaios River and choose a place for coffee. If you would like to explore further, add the streets of Varousi or the castle area, depending on your time and the pace of your group.
  2. Leave time for a meal and a breakA relaxed stop between sightseeing and the next activity keeps your day flexible. For families, we suggest fewer stops and more time at each one.
  3. Visit the Mill during its opening seasonCheck the official programme before choosing a day and time. If there is a particular event you want to attend, plan your journey and meal around it.
  4. Return to MouzakiFinish the day at your apartment. A fitted kitchen gives you flexibility for a light meal and breakfast before the next day’s trip.

The Mill of Elves is a seasonal attraction

The Mill of Elves takes place in Matsopoulos Park in Trikala during the Christmas season. Check the official event website for dates, hours, activities and access information.

Check the weather, choose comfortable footwear and prepare for time outdoors. The organisers determine opening hours and the availability of individual activities.

Before choosing your stay

Are the apartments in Trikala itself?

NP-Apt and GP-Apt are in Mouzaki, Karditsa. Visiting Trikala and Matsopoulos Park involves a journey. Use the directions above to decide whether the location suits your itinerary.

How many guests can stay in each apartment?

Each apartment welcomes up to 4 guests in total, with at least 1 adult. Enter children’s ages when calculating the cost.

Is the garden private?

Garden access is shared between the two accommodations. Each reservation is for the apartment you choose.

How do I send a booking request?

Open NP-Apt or GP-Apt, choose your dates and guests and calculate the indicative cost. Send your request and we will contact you to finalize the reservation. You can also use Booking.com or Airbnb.
